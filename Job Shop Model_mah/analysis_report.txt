Job Shop Model Simulation Analysis Report
=======================================

1. Queue Length Analysis:
   - Maximum queue length at WS1: 5.50
   - Maximum queue length at WS2: 6.50
   - Queue length increases exponentially with traffic intensity

2. Server Utilization Analysis:
   - Maximum utilization at WS1: 0.90
   - Maximum utilization at WS2: 0.99
   - Utilization increases linearly with traffic intensity

3. Delay Analysis:
   - Maximum queue delay at WS1: 11.80
   - Maximum queue delay at WS2: 13.40
   - Maximum system delay at WS1: 16.80
   - Maximum system delay at WS2: 20.40
   - Delays increase significantly as traffic intensity approaches 1

4. Bottleneck Analysis:
   - Primary bottleneck: Workstation 2
   - Bottleneck occurs due to:
     * Fewer servers (2 vs 3)
     * Longer service time (7.0 vs 5.0)

5. Recommendations:
   - Consider adding more servers to Workstation 2
   - Implement load balancing between workstations
   - Optimize service times at Workstation 2
   - Consider implementing priority scheduling for critical jobs
